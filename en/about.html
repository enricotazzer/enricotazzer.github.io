Hi, I'm Enrico Tazzer, a student on the double master's degree in Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity at the University of Udine and the Alpen-Adria-Universität Klagenfurt.

My studies lean towards applying AI in medicine, and more generally towards applications that move away from the usual image and video generation (which, frankly, I'm a bit fed up with). Despite the incredible results there, I think we've reached the point where further progress in that area is of little practical use. What I want is to push, in my own small way, the field where these tools can have a real impact: making a healthcare system that, let's say, leaves a lot to be desired considerably more efficient.

I'm a very curious person by nature, and I like getting my hands dirty with new activities and fields, even ones far from what I study. So on this blog, besides my study material (lol), you'll find articles written and edited by yours truly (yes, I'm a bit self-centered) about Artificial Intelligence in its broadest sense, along with pieces on whatever topics and news catch my interest.
