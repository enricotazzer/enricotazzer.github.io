ORACLE — Oncology Reconstruction And Clinical Learning Engine

An end-to-end deep learning pipeline for brain tumor analysis: segment tumors in multimodal MRI, reconstruct full 3D brain volumes from sparse slice observations via GAN-based generation, then predict tumor growth with a Physics-Informed Neural Network — complete with uncertainty quantification (MC-Dropout, Deep Ensemble, Laplace posterior).

Medical AI · v1 Complete

Chest X-Ray Triage Assistant with Explainability

A multi-label classifier that estimates clinical urgency from chest X-rays and produces a Grad-CAM visual explanation for every decision, built to re-prioritize a radiology reading queue so urgent cases surface first. Includes a self-critical audit of a known dataset spurious-correlation risk and a subgroup fairness analysis.
